# Product Overview

## Our Products

We offer a comprehensive suite of AI-powered solutions designed to help businesses automate customer support and improve customer satisfaction.

### AI Chatbot Widget

Our flagship product is an embeddable AI chatbot widget that can be integrated into any website. The chatbot uses advanced natural language processing to understand customer questions and provide accurate, contextual responses based on your company's documentation.

**Key Features:**

- Easy integration with any website
- Powered by local AI models for data privacy
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for accurate responses
- Real-time conversation management
- Source attribution for transparency

### Smart Document Indexing

Our document indexing system automatically processes your company documentation and creates a searchable knowledge base. The system supports multiple document formats including Markdown, PDF, and plain text.

**Benefits:**

- Automatic semantic search
- Duplicate question detection
- Usage analytics and insights
- Continuous learning from customer interactions

### Analytics Dashboard

Track chatbot performance, identify common customer questions, and gain insights into customer needs through our comprehensive analytics dashboard.

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Question frequency and trends
- Response accuracy
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Knowledge base coverage
