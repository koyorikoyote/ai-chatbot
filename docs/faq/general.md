# General FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an AI chatbot?

An AI chatbot is an automated conversational agent that uses artificial intelligence to understand and respond to customer questions. Our chatbot uses advanced natural language processing and retrieval-augmented generation to provide accurate, contextual responses based on your company's documentation.

### How does the chatbot work?

The chatbot works in several steps:

1. Customer asks a question through the chat widget
2. The question is converted into a semantic embedding
3. Our system searches your knowledge base for relevant information
4. The AI generates a natural response using the retrieved context
5. The response is displayed to the customer with source references

### Is my data secure?

Yes, data security is our top priority. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We offer on-premise deployment options for Enterprise customers who require complete data control. Our system can run entirely locally without sending data to external services.

### What languages are supported?

Currently, we support English. Additional language support is planned for future releases.

### Can I customize the chatbot's appearance?

Yes, you can customize the widget's colors, position, title, and welcome message to match your brand identity.

### How accurate are the responses?

Response accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of your knowledge base. Our system achieves over 85% accuracy when properly configured with comprehensive documentation.

### What happens if the chatbot doesn't know the answer?

If the chatbot cannot find relevant information in your knowledge base, it will politely inform the customer and suggest contacting human support.

### Can I see what questions customers are asking?

Yes, our analytics dashboard shows all customer questions, including frequency, trends, and common topics.

### How long does it take to set up?

Basic setup takes just a few minutes. Simply add our script tag to your website and configure your preferences. Indexing your documentation may take additional time depending on the volume.

### Do I need technical knowledge to use this?

No technical knowledge is required for basic setup and usage. However, some technical knowledge may be helpful for advanced customization and integration.

### Can I try before I buy?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You can test all features and see how the chatbot works with your documentation.

### What if I exceed my plan limits?

If you exceed your conversation limit, you can either upgrade your plan or purchase additional conversations as an add-on. We'll notify you before you reach your limit.
